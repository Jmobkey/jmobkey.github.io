    What's missing

    This is a self-assessment, so it is only as honest as your answers. If you are unsure about a statement, answer "Partly". If nobody can answer it at all, that is the finding.

    Choices worth knowing about. The verdict follows your weakest area rather than the average, because a project fails at its weakest link. Each area has three statements on a four-point scale, so scores are coarse. The thresholds are arbitrary and meant to be argued with.

    The six areas come from experience of what stalls AI projects after a successful pilot, not from a published standard. This doesn't replace a security, legal or technical review.

    A strong average hides the one thing that stops the project. That's why the verdict ignores it.

    Runs in your browser. Answers are never sent; a result link carries them in the web address only.
