Josh Murphy / AI strategy and delivery

Notes and tools from the gap between an AI strategy and an AI deployment.

Getting AI into production inside a large organisation turns out to be a problem of data access, process reality and ownership far more than a problem of models.

This is where I put the practical things. Everything here is free, runs entirely in your browser, and collects nothing.

What a workload costs once it leaves the pilot. Models the multipliers that don't reach the business case: retrieved context on every call, conversation carried across turns, retries, evals, and non-production traffic.

More as I build it.

Why

Most writing about enterprise AI is either vendor marketing or strategy at an altitude where nothing can go wrong. There isn't much covering the unglamorous middle, where the questions are about who owns the data, what the process actually looks like, and who maintains the thing in a year.

These are my attempt at that middle. They're opinionated on purpose, and each one says plainly what it doesn't account for.

My own views rather than any employer's, offered as thinking aids rather than professional advice.
